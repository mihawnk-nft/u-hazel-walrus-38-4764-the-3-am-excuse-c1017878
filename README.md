## What it does

`main.py` (pure Python standard library, no dependencies) writes one line
under 200 characters to `/output/entry.txt`: the most creative excuse a
hardware wallet gives for refusing to sign a transaction at 3 AM.

> It's 3 AM. My secure element is off the clock, your 'genius' trade is
> already filed under Regrets, and I don't sign anything the sun hasn't
> seen yet.

## Why this line

- **On subject:** a hardware wallet refusing to sign at 3 AM, in the
  device's own first-person voice.
- **Original angle:** no battery, firmware-update or cold-storage jokes —
  the secure element is "off the clock" and files the trade under
  "Regrets" before it even happens.
- **The hour is the joke:** the line only works at 3 AM — it refuses to
  sign anything "the sun hasn't seen yet", so it could not be pasted
  onto a 3 PM transaction.
- **Tight:** 149 characters, one breath, no explanation of its own joke.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt
```
